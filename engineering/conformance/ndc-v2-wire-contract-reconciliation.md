## 2. Legend for matrix cells

- `D0 §n`, `D1 §n`, `D2 §n`, `D1-am §n`, `Profile 1 Decision X.n` cite section or enumerated item
  numbers in the named authority. "Not named" means the authority states no requirement for the
  cited field; it is not a permission to omit or invent one.
- `FB:` = `fieldbook:src/distribution/ndc_release.rs`; `NZ:` = `nirnay:crates/release-verifier/src/`;
  `NZT:` = `nirnay:crates/release-verifier/tests/verifier.rs`; `V:` =
  `engineering/conformance/ndc-v2-conformance-vectors/`; `AM §1 row n` =
  `engineering/conformance/ndc-v2-acceptance-matrix.md` section 1.
- `NONE` means no test vector or executed cross-repository check covers that field at the exact
  heads. A proposed case in a `Test vector` cell is a review request, not existing evidence.
- Role codes are those registered in `engineering/conformance/ndc-v2-conformance-matrix.md` §1:
  FPO producer owner, NCO consumer owner, NDC contract owner, DRS data-rights steward, SKC
  signing-key custodian, OWN programme owner. Every representation decision remains AWAITING
  OWNER DECISION even where a role is named as decision authority.
- `Recommended canonical representation` is a review proposal at schema level. Neither Rust
  type is selected as normative; where an authority already ratifies a wire rule, the cell
  records that rule instead of proposing a new one.
